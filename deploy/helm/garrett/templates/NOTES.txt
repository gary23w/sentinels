garrett is starting on every node{{ if .Values.nodeSelector }} that matches the node selector{{ end }}, one pod each, in the namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

Each node enrols itself with your service the first time its pod starts (mode {{ .Values.mode }}), and its log says so:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} -o wide
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs -l app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }} --prefix

A node keeps its credential in /var/lib/garrett-node, through upgrades and after helm uninstall, and does not enrol
again while it has one.
